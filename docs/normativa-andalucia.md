# The Andalusian rules Rampa has to live inside

Researched 30 August 2026, because `017` could not be written without it. Kept
here rather than inside a specification: several specifications depend on these
facts and they change when the Consejería changes them, not when we do.

**This is a working summary by a non-lawyer, from public sources. It is not legal
advice and must be checked with somebody who does this for a living before any of
it is relied on in front of a school.**

## The two documents, and the line between them

| | **ACNS** — adaptación curricular **no significativa** | **ACS** — adaptación curricular **significativa** |
|---|---|---|
| What it may change | Methodology, activities, timing, materials, assessment instruments. It «**no afectará** a la consecución de las competencias clave, objetivos y criterios de evaluación» of the programación didáctica | It **modifies** objetivos and criterios de evaluación, and consequently the nuclear contents |
| Who it is for | A learner with NEAE showing a **desfase curricular of at least one course** in the subject being adapted | A learner with **necesidades educativas especiales** |
| Who authors it | **Coordinated by the tutor**, who completes every section *except* the propuesta curricular, completed by the teacher of the subject being adapted | The **profesorado especialista en educación especial**, with the subject teacher, advised by the Equipo/Departamento de Orientación |
| What it requires first | — | A **prior evaluación psicopedagógica** by the equipo or departamento de orientación |
| Where it lives | **Séneca** | **Séneca**, as the DIAC (Documento Individual de Adaptación Curricular) |
| Promotion and certification | Referenced to the centre's own Proyecto Educativo criteria | Adapted, in line with the propuesta curricular's objectives |

Regulated by the **Instrucciones de 8 de marzo de 2017 de la Dirección General de
Participación y Equidad**, which update the Protocolo de Detección, Identificación
del alumnado con NEAE y Organización de la Respuesta Educativa (itself established
by the Instrucciones de 22 de junio de 2015).

## What an ACS document must contain

At minimum, per the regulation:

1. A **propuesta curricular por áreas o materias**, recording the modification of
   objetivos, metodología, contenidos, criterios de evaluación, and the
   organisation of space and time.
2. **Adaptation of the criteria for promotion and certification**, consistent with
   the objectives of that propuesta curricular.
3. **Seguimiento y valoración** of the learner's progress, with information given
   to the learner and to the family.

## Why this matters to this project, in one line each

**Principle III is the statutory boundary.** «Adapt the how, never the what» is,
word for word, what separates an ACNS from an ACS. Everything Rampa does today
falls inside the ACNS definition. The interface has never used the term.

**Carlos's «copilot para el PT y el Tutor» is two products, and the law splits
them.** The tutor coordinates the ACNS. The PT authors the ACS. Blurring them puts
a tutor's name on a decision that requires a psychopedagogical report.

**Séneca is the register.** Not us. Anything Rampa produces is material she carries
there, and a Rampa document that looks filed is a defect.

**The evaluación psicopedagógica is the gate on the ACS**, and it belongs to
Orientación. Rampa must never produce one, summarise one it was not given, or
produce a document that reads as though one exists — an ACS without it is
procedurally void, and a complete-looking draft invites somebody to file it.

**A DIAC is the most sensitive document in the system.** It contains the
psychopedagogical findings and, in practice, a diagnosis.
[ADR 0002](decisions/0002-no-clinical-material.md) says Rampa holds no clinical
material. `017` resolves this as *read, extract the measures, keep only those* —
and states honestly that a page image sent for reading contains everything printed
on that page.

## The curriculum itself

The Andalusian curriculum — competencias específicas, criterios de evaluación,
saberes básicos, descriptores operativos, with their official codes — is published
in the **Orden de 30 de mayo de 2023** (BOJA23-104-00208-9731-01), under LOMLOE
(BOE-A-2020-17264).

It is public legislation. `011` FR-918 should be loaded from the boletín, not from
another application's copy of it — cleaner, and it generalises: another comunidad
is another boletín, which is exactly what `011`'s corpus design is for. See
[sda-ia-junta-andalucia.md](sda-ia-junta-andalucia.md).
