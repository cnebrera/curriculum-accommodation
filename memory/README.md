# Your memory folder

This is where Rampa keeps what you have taught it. **These files are yours.**

Nothing in this folder is uploaded anywhere, and nothing here is committed to git
— except this README. Updating Rampa (`git pull`) never touches your files.

## What is in here

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `house.md` | How *you* work: fonts, conventions, things your school always does |
| `journal/` | What went wrong or right on a specific job, and why |
| `index.md` | Generated. Lets the agent find relevant entries without reading everything |
| `archive/` | Entries that have been superseded or already acted on |

Your learners' profiles and notes live in `profiles/`, not here.

## Backing it up

Copy two folders: `memory/` and `profiles/`. That is the whole backup. They are
plain text, so any backup tool, USB stick or cloud folder works, and you can read
them without Rampa.

If a learner changes school or teacher, `/rampa-memory export` packages their
profile and notes to hand over.

## One rule when writing here

Write the **pattern**, not the passage. "Splitting at commas lost the causal
link" is useful and safe. Pasting the worksheet paragraph is neither — it is
copyrighted material, and it can identify a child.

## Making it better over time

Run `/rampa-memory` every few weeks. It reviews what has accumulated and proposes
promoting the things that keep repeating: into a learner's profile, into your
house style, or into a recipe improvement for everyone. You confirm each one.

That last path — a recipe improvement — is how your experience reaches other
teachers. It is rewritten as a general statement first, with everything about
your learner and your material removed, and you see it before it goes anywhere.
