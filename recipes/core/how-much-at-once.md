# How much is on the page at once

**Not yet reviewed by a practising teacher.** Written 2026-09-04 from
`instructions/axes.md` and the gap the review found (PROD-01, decision P1): `ATE`
alone activated **nothing** — `one-task-per-page` needs `COG>=2` *and* `ATE>=2`, so a
learner whose only barrier is how long he lasts got an unadapted sheet. Treat what
follows as a starting point a PT should correct.

## What this axis is

`ATE>=2` is «cuánto rato aguanta». Not whether he understands it and not whether he
can read it: whether he is still there at exercise four. The failure looks like
carelessness and is not — the first three are right and the rest is abandoned or
guessed.

## What to do

- **Fewer things per page, and an end he can see.** «Quedan dos» is worth more than
  any encouragement: a task with a visible edge is a task worth starting.
- **Number what there is and say how many.** «Ejercicio 2 de 4» at the top, so
  finishing is a fact and not a hope.
- **A break written into the sheet**, where the sheet is long: a line that says «aquí
  puedes parar y seguir después» is permission he will otherwise not take.
- **Nothing on the page that is not the task.** No decorative image, no sidebar, no
  «¿sabías que…?». Everything on the paper competes.
- **The hardest thing first**, while there is attention to spend on it — unless
  `works` says he needs a win to start, in which case `works` wins (rule 2 of the
  conflict order).
- **Cut length before cutting content.** The same ten sums over two sheets is the
  same practice; five sums is a different exercise, and on an exam it is a change of
  criterion.

## Before

> **4.** Resuelve estas diez restas. Cuando acabes, colorea el dibujo y busca en la
> sopa de letras las cinco palabras del tema.

## After

> *(hoja 1 de 2 · ejercicio 4)*
>
> **4.** Resuelve estas cinco restas. Cuando acabes, para: la hoja 2 es para después.
>
> *(hoja 2 de 2 · ejercicio 4, sigue)*
>
> **4.** Estas cinco y ya está.

## Anti-patterns

- **Removing exercises to make it shorter.** That is less practice, not less load —
  and on an assessment it is not yours to decide.
- **Adding a reward to the page.** A sticker to colour is one more thing on the
  paper, which is the barrier this recipe is about.
- **«Un poco más y acabas» with no number.** Vague encouragement asks him to trust
  an estimate; a count is a fact.
- **Splitting the difference: five per page but the page still busy.** The blank
  space *is* the adaptation.
- **Renumbering across the split.** Hard rule 7: the class works out loud on
  «el ejercicio cuatro», and both halves stay 4.
