# When what is said does not arrive

**Not yet reviewed by a practising teacher.** Written 2026-09-04 from
`instructions/axes.md` and the gap the review found (PROD-01, decision P1): **no
recipe in the corpus mentioned `PER-A` at all**, so a deaf learner — `PER-A:3`, «lo
hablado no le llega» — activated nothing whatsoever. Treat what follows as a starting
point a PT should correct.

## What this axis is

`PER-A>=2` is «no basta con hablado: también por escrito». At 3 it is «lo hablado no
le llega». The sheet is not the problem; **what surrounds the sheet is**. A worksheet
that is perfectly legible still fails him if the thing that makes it doable was said
out loud once, at the front, before he had it in his hands.

## What to do

- **Nothing important exists only in speech.** Anything said aloud that the task
  depends on — what to do, how many, how long, what «como el ejemplo» refers to — is
  on the page in writing.
- **Write the instruction the teacher would say.** Not «haz los ejercicios» but the
  sentence she actually says while handing it out: «Haz los tres primeros. El cuarto
  lo vemos juntos.»
- **Say where the answer goes**, in writing. «Contesta debajo», «rodea», «une con
  flechas» — the convention that is obvious once you have heard it and invisible when
  you have not.
- **A visible sequence for anything with steps**, numbered, so the order does not
  depend on having heard it.
- **Anything with sound gets its text.** A video, an audio exercise, a dictation: the
  transcript travels with the sheet, or the exercise is not accessible and the sheet
  says so rather than pretending.
- **Never «pregunta si no lo entiendes».** Asking is the channel that does not work.

## Anti-patterns

- **Reading the instruction aloud twice.** Louder is not written.
- **Assuming lip-reading covers it.** It costs continuous attention and it fails the
  moment the teacher turns to the board.
- **Sign language in the material.** If he uses it, that is a person's job in the
  room, not a drawing on a photocopy — and guessing which variant would be worse than
  not trying.
- **Removing the listening exercise.** On an exam that is a change of criterion and
  not yours (`exam-access-not-difficulty`): flag it and stop.
- Ignoring that `PER-A` and `DEC` can both be high. Written instead of spoken is no
  help if the writing is a wall — then it is this recipe **and** `decoding-load`.
